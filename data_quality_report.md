## Interpretation and limits

Direct changes use only the four consistently worded indicators (women’s literacy, schooling, marriage before 18, and clean cooking fuel) and name-matched districts outside Harvard Appendix A. The appendix provides no complete parent crosswalk, so its 130 entries (128 unique district IDs) are excluded. Name normalization is not proof of boundary identity; these are candidate direct comparisons. Sanitation is excluded because the two rounds use different denominators. Neither factsheet source contains the requested bank-account-use or mobile-phone indicators, so the empowerment index and finance-linked typology remain unavailable.
