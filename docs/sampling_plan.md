# Field validation sampling plan

## Purpose
Validate the difference between account ownership and women’s independent, recent use, and identify practical service barriers.

## District selection
Use Shrawasti, Lalitpur, Bahraich, Balrampur and Sitapur as provisional candidates from the NFHS-5 mean-rank composite of literacy, schooling, early marriage (reverse-oriented), and clean-fuel levels. Confirm boundary fit, field access, local partner capacity, and financial-access barriers before final selection. The composite is not a financial inclusion index. Source: outputs/tables/up_district_rankings.csv.

## Proposed design
Within each selected district, sample 4 blocks, 2 villages per block, and 15 eligible women per village (target 120 interviews per district; 360–600 total). Stratify villages by rural/urban setting where feasible. Within villages, use a documented household listing and random selection; interview one eligible woman per selected household. These are proposed operational parameters, not a power calculation. Revisit after district population frames and field budget are known.

## Eligibility and safeguards
Women aged 18–49 are the proposed participants. Obtain informed consent, conduct interviews privately, allow skipping any question, do not collect names or account numbers, and provide a local referral sheet for complaints and financial fraud concerns. Ensure Hindi and locally understood language support.

## Analysis
Report weighted and unweighted proportions separately where sampling weights are available. Present ownership, recent use, independent control, transaction frequency, and access barriers as distinct measures. Compare self-reported use with available service mapping; do not infer causality.
