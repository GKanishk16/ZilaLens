# Qualitative policy review

The policy review uses the official PMJDY Mission Document/Brochure (2014) and the NITI Aayog/UNDP *Aspirational Districts Programme: An Appraisal*. Seven short paraphrased passages are recorded in `outputs/tables/policy_coding_matrix.xlsx`; extracted page text is retained in `data/raw/docs/` alongside the PDFs.

The PMJDY document describes extending banking reach through branches and Bank Mitras (physical p. 18), interoperable transactions and service points (p. 20), and flags unreliable telecom networks in tribal and hilly areas (p. 30). The ADP appraisal describes district capacity building and continued needs (physical p. 15), the convergence/collaboration/competition model (p. 20), and its choice of pre-pandemic observations for its own analysis (p. 28).

These sources support a design-level discussion of access infrastructure, connectivity, and implementation capacity. They do not establish that these factors caused the observed NFHS changes. NFHS district comparison and convergence with quantitative patterns remain pending the official NFHS-5 workbook. The crosswalk in `outputs/tables/convergence_table.csv` makes this gap explicit.
